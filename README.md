# 30-Day Calisthenics Program & Exercise Library

A progressive, zero-equipment bodyweight training plan and visual exercise directory for beginners, built with the **Swiss International Typographic Style (Vignelli)** and grounded in **[Squatwolf's Beginner Calisthenics Guide](https://squatwolf.com/blogs/fitness/calisthenics-beginners)**.

---

## Quick Start

Open [`calisthenics-plan.html`](./calisthenics-plan.html) or [`index.html`](./index.html) in any modern web browser:
- **Interactive Day Tracker**: Check off days as you complete them (persisted via `localStorage`).
- **Real-Time Progress Bar**: Tracks completion count out of 30 days.
- **Exercise Photo Lightbox**: Click any exercise name to preview images directly from its folder.
- **Meal Timing Schedule**: Pre- and post-workout nutritional windows.
- **Spine Stability Protocol**: 6 dedicated drills protecting the lumbar spine and joints.
- **3-Month Progression Roadmap**: Long-term skill roadmap beyond Day 30.

---

## How to Add Demonstration Photos

Each subfolder in this directory corresponds to one exercise. Drop your reference photos or gifs into the matching folder:

### Supported Filenames:
- `guide.jpg` / `guide.png` / `guide.webp` / `guide.gif`
- `01.jpg` through `10.jpg` (step-by-step progressions)
- `starting-position.jpg`, `midpoint.jpg`, `end-position.jpg`
- `step-1.jpg`, `step-2.jpg`, `step-3.jpg`
- `front.jpg`, `side.jpg`, `form.jpg`

Once files are placed into any folder, clicking that exercise in `calisthenics-plan.html` will automatically find and render them in a clean Swiss-style popup gallery.

## 8 Principles for Beginners (Squatwolf Protocol)

1. **Form Over Repetitions**: 5 clean reps beat 15 sagging reps.
2. **The 5-Minute Warmup**: Never skip joint lubrication and core activation.
3. **Progressive Overload**: Advance volume, hold times, and lever angles systematically.
4. **Mind-Muscle Connection**: Intentionally contract target muscles during each rep.
5. **Active Listening & Rest**: Hypertrophy and tendon remodeling occur during sleep and rest.
6. **Spinal Stability Focus**: Prevent lower back sag through deep anti-extension drills.
7. **Consistent Nourishment**: Carbohydrates 60–90 min pre-workout; protein within 45 min post-workout.
8. **Habit Over Motivation**: Show up daily, record scores, and build sustainable habits.

## 3-Month Roadmap Beyond Day 30

- **Month 01 (Completed)**: Foundational Push-Ups (3&times;10), Deep Squats (3&times;25), Forearm Plank (60s), Dead Hang (30s).
- **Month 02 (Reinforcement)**: Pike Push-Ups (4&times;8 for shoulder power), Bar/Door Pull-Ups (4&times;6–10), Parallel/Chair Dips (4&times;12), Hollow Body (4&times;30s).
- **Month 03 (Advanced Skills)**: Wall Handstand Holds (3&times;30s), Floor Tuck L-Sits (3&times;15s), Assisted Pistol Squats (3&times;5 each), Archer Push-Ups (3&times;6 each).
